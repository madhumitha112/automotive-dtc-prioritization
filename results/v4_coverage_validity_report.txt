6. VALIDITY LIMITATIONS
==============================================================================
1. V4 is a controlled synthetic benchmark, not data recorded from physical vehicles.
2. The 24 cases consist of 12 scenario templates repeated twice with different signal noise.
3. Repetitions within a template do not constitute independent scenario families.
4. Each case contains two candidates. The analysis does not establish performance with larger candidate sets.
5. The candidate list is assumed to be supplied by an upstream diagnostic layer. DTC detection is not evaluated.
6. Pairwise coverage is limited to the candidate combinations represented in V4.
7. P0335 uses an indirect RPM-instability proxy; a direct crankshaft-position waveform is not represented.
8. Ranking results are benchmark-specific and do not establish real-vehicle diagnostic performance.
9. No dataset, threshold, context weight or ranking rule was changed in this analysis.
