INTERPRETATION LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Context scores represent threshold-based evidence fractions, not calibrated fault probabilities.
2. Primary-versus-competing score comparisons are descriptive and do not establish statistical significance.
3. Ablation measures the effect of zeroing one DTC's context contribution while retaining its static priority.
4. A rule's ranking contribution is specific to this V4 benchmark and its candidate combinations.
5. The V4 benchmark contains 12 scenario templates with two repetitions each; repetitions are not independent real-world vehicle trials.
6. P0335 evidence uses RPM instability as an indirect proxy, not a direct crankshaft-position waveform.
7. No thresholds, weights, decoding definitions or ranking rules were tuned in this audit.
