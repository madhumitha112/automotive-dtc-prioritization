5. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. V3 and V4 are benchmark versions with different case counts and benchmark designs.
2. V4 uses 12 scenario templates repeated twice; the repeated cases should not be treated as independent scenario families.
3. V4 is a controlled synthetic benchmark and does not represent recordings from physical vehicles.
4. The comparison measures ranking performance conditional on the supplied candidate DTC set. It does not evaluate DTC detection.
5. Top-3 accuracy is not suitable as a primary cross-version comparison because V3 includes three-candidate cases while V4 has two candidates per case.
6. Differences between V3 and V4 performance cannot be attributed solely to benchmark size. Scenario composition and candidate-pair composition also differ.
7. These results do not establish generalization to real vehicles or unseen diagnostic conditions.
8. No ranking rules, context thresholds, static priorities or context weights were changed.
